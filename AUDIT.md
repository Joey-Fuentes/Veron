# Audit model

The **seven audit criteria** are defined in `ARCHITECTURE.md` §3. Their
concrete realization landed as the **substage record schema** — `[[substage]]`
TOML with sha256 + exact bytes + mode per input/output, literal-argv steps,
and falsifiable builder edges — documented in `docs/DESIGN.md` (D4), with the
criteria-to-field mapping in [`docs/AUDIT.md`](./docs/AUDIT.md). Records are
generated by builds, frozen in `stages/<n>/substages*.toml`, and enforced by
compare gates; `ledger/` is their generated, signed concatenation at stage 6.
