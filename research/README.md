# research/

Point-in-time research reports, kept verbatim and dated.

They are here because the decisions they informed are recorded elsewhere in
short form, and a summary is not checkable. When a decision looks wrong later,
the question is usually "what did we know when we made it" — and that is only
answerable if the reasoning survives alongside the conclusion.

**They age, and they are not corrected in place.** A report describes what was
true on its date. If something in one turns out to be wrong or has since
changed, the correction goes in the document that cites it — `AUTHENTICATION.md`
for both of the current reports — and says so explicitly. Editing a report to
match what we later learned would destroy the only record of what we actually
knew at the time.
